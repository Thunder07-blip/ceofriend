# 🧭 User Flow & Experience (user_flow.md)
## Predictive Engineering Intelligence Platform

---

## 🎯 Goal

Design a user journey that:

- Feels **intelligent and real-time**
- Requires **minimal effort from the user**
- Delivers **maximum business insight quickly**
- Translates complexity → **clear decisions**

---

## 🧠 Core UX Philosophy

> “From Repository → To Revenue Risk → To Decision”

---

## 👤 Target User

- CEO  
- Founder  
- Product Manager  
- Non-technical stakeholder  

---

## ⚡ Key UX Principle

👉 User should understand **business impact within 30 seconds**

---

# ⚡ UX DESIGN PRINCIPLES

---

## 1. Show Value Early

- Show ₹ impact ASAP  

---

## 2. Progressive Disclosure

- Summary → details  

---

## 3. Visual Hierarchy

- Big numbers → attention  
- Colors → urgency  

---

## 4. Real-Time Feedback

- No blank screens  
- Always show progress  

---

## 5. Business First

- Money > metrics
